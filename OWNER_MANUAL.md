# 👑 ZERENELLE — Atelier Owner's Private Control Guide

> **100% Stealth & Private Control**: Customers and public visitors will **NEVER** see any admin buttons or links on your public website. 

---

## 🔒 How to Access Your Private Admin Portal

You have two secret ways to open your control portal:

### Method 1: Direct Private URL (Recommended)
Simply type `/admin.html` in your browser address bar:
- If running locally: `http://localhost:3000/admin.html` (or open the file `admin.html`)
- When your website is live online: `www.zerenelle.com/admin.html`

### Method 2: Stealth Keyboard Shortcut
When viewing your main public storefront (`index.html`), simply press:
👉 **`Ctrl + Shift + A`** (or **`Cmd + Shift + A`** on a Mac).  
Your browser will instantly navigate to your private portal!

---

## 🔑 Logging In to the Portal

1. When you open `admin.html`, you will be greeted by the **Security Gate**.
2. Enter your Master Owner PIN:
   - **Default PIN**: `1234`
3. Click **"Unlock Atelier Dashboard"**.
4. You are now inside your full management suite!

---

## 🏷️ 1. Increasing or Decreasing Prices

1. In `admin.html`, scroll to **"Inventory & Pricing"**.
2. **Quick Price Edit**:
   - Next to any bouquet, you will see a box with `₹ [ 1699 ]`.
   - Type your new price (e.g. `1850` or `1499`).
   - Click outside the box. It **immediately updates** on the live public storefront, in shopping carts, and in WhatsApp order summaries!
3. **Set a Discount / Strikethrough Price**:
   - Click **"✏️ Edit"** on any bouquet.
   - Enter your **Selling Price in ₹** (e.g. `1499`).
   - Enter your **Original Price in ₹** (e.g. `1799`).
   - Click **"Save Changes to Store"**. Customers will see ~~₹1,799~~ **₹1,499**!

---

## 📷 2. Changing Product Photos or Names

1. Click the **"✏️ Edit"** button next to any bouquet in the list.
2. In the editor window:
   - **Change the Name**: Type the new name in the **Product Title** field.
   - **Upload a New Photo**: Click **"Upload New Photo from Device 📷"** and pick any picture taken from your phone camera or computer. It previews instantly!
   - **Edit Scent, Stems, or Description**: Customize any details you wish.
3. Click **"Save Changes to Store"**. It updates live on your public website!

---

## ➕ 3. Introducing a Brand New Bouquet

1. In `admin.html`, go to **"Add a New Bouquet Creation"**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Bouquet Name** (e.g., *"Scarlet Velvet Peony Deluxe"*)
   - **Selling Price in ₹** (e.g., `1750`)
   - **Category** (*Romance & Roses*, *Pastel Meadow*, *Sunflowers & Cheer*, or *Mini Desk Pots*)
   - **Badge** (e.g., *"New Launch"*, *"Limited Edition"*, etc.)
   - **Product Photo**: Click **"Upload from Device 📷"** to pick a photo from your phone or PC.
   - **Stems & Description**: A short note about the flowers and velvet wire used.
3. Click **"Publish Bouquet to Live Website"**.
4. The new bouquet immediately goes live on the public site!

---

## 🗑️ 4. Removing / Deleting a Bouquet

1. Find the bouquet under **"Manage Existing Bouquets"**.
2. Click the red **"🗑️ Delete"** button.
3. Confirm when prompted, and it is instantly removed from the public store.

---

## 📢 5. Changing Banner Offers & Your WhatsApp Number

In Section 1:
- **Top Announcement Banner**: Type whatever text you want visitors to see (e.g., *"✨ Festive Special: Flat 10% off with code WELCOME10 ✨"*).
- **Owner WhatsApp Number**: Put your actual phone number with country code (e.g. `919876543210`) so 1-click orders arrive straight to your smartphone.
- **Prepaid UPI ID**: Update your GPay/PhonePe ID (`yourname@okhdfcbank`).
- Click **"Save Settings & Banner Offer"**.

---

## 💾 6. Backups & Data Protection

- **Backup**: Click **"⬇️ Backup Catalog (JSON)"** to download a safety copy of all your custom products and prices.
- **Restore**: Click **"⬆️ Restore Backup"** anytime you switch browsers or computers.
