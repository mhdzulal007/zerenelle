# 🌸 ZERENELLE (Kerala) — Brand Strategy & Business Masterplan

> **"Everlasting Blooms, Handcrafted with Love in Kerala"**  
> *The Practical Blueprint for a Small-to-Medium Handcrafted Chenille Floral Atelier*

---

## 1. Business Vision & Core Constraints

- **Brand Name**: **Zerenelle** (Artisan Chenille Floral Atelier)
- **Scale**: Small to Medium scale boutique business, focusing on **bespoke, customized made-to-order floral work**.
- **Delivery Scope**: **All Kerala Delivery Only** (All 14 districts covered: Ernakulam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Thrissur, Kottayam, Kannur, Kollam, Malappuram, Palakkad, Alappuzha, Kasaragod, Pathanamthitta, Idukki, Wayanad).
- **Payment Policy**: **100% Prepaid Orders Only (No COD)** via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or Bank Transfer.
  - *Why No COD is strictly necessary*: Every bouquet is customized with specific colors, stem counts, recipient name, and calligraphy cards. Personalized handmade crafts cannot be refused on doorstep delivery.
- **Sales Funnel Model**:
  - The website serves as a **high-end brand showcase, interactive customizer, and customer magnet**.
  - Orders and customization consultations flow directly into **WhatsApp Chat Orders** (and online booking records) where personal rapport is built.

## 3. Delivery & Courier Logistics Across Kerala

1. **Courier Partners in Kerala**:
   - **DTDC / Professional Couriers / Speed Post (India Post)**: Delivery across all 14 districts in 24–48 hours for South/Central Kerala, and 48–72 hours for Northern Kerala.
   - Standard shipping cost inside Kerala: ₹80 – ₹120 for rigid cylindrical boxes.
   - **Pricing Strategy**: Charge standard ₹120 shipping, and offer **FREE Delivery on orders above ₹1,999**!
2. **Packaging Best Practices**:
   - Pack bouquets inside a **rigid cylinder presentation tube** or a sturdy double-wall carton with internal bubble-wrap and foam anchors.
   - Include a cute, printed **"Fluff & Care Card"**:
     > *"Welcome your Zerenelle Bouquet! Because our blooms are crafted with high-density velvet wire, feel free to gently fan out or bend any petals into your preferred arrangement. Keep away from water and direct soaking rain."*

## 5. Marketing & Customer Acquisition Playbook for Kerala

1. **Instagram & Reels Strategy**:
   - Audio hook: Satisfying ASMR sound of snipping craft wire, curling velvet petals, and spraying lavender mist.
   - Malayalam / English caption hooks:
     - *"Fresh flowers wilt in 3 days. Give a bouquet that stays in their room for 30 years 🌸"*
     - *"Graduation gifting idea in Kerala: The everlasting Sunflower teddy bouquet 🎓"*
2. **Campus & College Ambassadors**:
   - Reach out to students at engineering, medical, and arts colleges across Kochi, Trivandrum, and Kozhikode for convocation/graduation season bouquets.
3. **Bridesmaid & Engagement Gifting**:
   - Partner with Kerala wedding planners and bridal makeover artists to offer everlasting bridal proposal boxes.
