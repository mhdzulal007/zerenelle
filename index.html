Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you deliver, and what is your payment policy? ▼

We deliver exclusively across all districts in Kerala via safe courier / Speed Post in rigid cylinder boxes. Because every bouquet is customized and handcrafted to your exact preferences, we operate on a 100% Prepaid basis (No Cash on Delivery) via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or Bank Transfer.

How long do Zerenelle pipe cleaner bouquets last? ▼

Forever! Unlike fresh flowers that wither in days, our high-density chenille wire never fades, rots, or loses its structure. Keep it away from prolonged direct soaking rain, and gently dust it once every few months with a cool hair dryer or soft brush to keep it fresh for decades.

How do you ship bouquets without crushing the fuzzy petals? ▼

Every bouquet is anchored inside an ultra-rigid cylinder presentation gift box. Even if a petal is gently pressed during transit, the beauty of chenille wire is flexibility: you can simply fluff or bend it back into shape in seconds!

How does the fragrance infusion work? ▼

We gently infuse the porous internal core of the stems with pure botanical essential oil mists (such as French Lavender, Wild Rosewater, or Madagascar Vanilla). The scent gently diffuses for 3 to 6 months. You can also re-spritz with your favorite perfume or essential oil whenever you wish!
